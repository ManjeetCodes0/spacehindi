A young 24-year-old astronomer (Clyde Tombaugh) sits hunched over a blink comparator — a brass and steel optical instrument with two eyepieces — comparing photographic plates of the night sky. The room is dimly lit by a single warm desk lamp. On the comparator's plates, one tiny dot shifts position between two exposures — the moment of Pluto's discovery. Stacks of glass photographic plates are neatly organized on wooden shelves behind him. Through the observatory window, the Arizona desert night sky blazes with stars. A handwritten note on the desk reads "Planet X?" The mood is quiet triumph — months of painstaking work culminating in a single shifted dot. Volumetric warm lamplight, dust motes suspended in air, rich chiaroscuro. Color palette: warm amber lamplight, cool blue starlight through window, dark mahogany wood, brass instrument gold, photographic plate silver. 8K, cinematic period drama quality, The Imitation Game visual tone.

Pluto dominates the left side — warm tan and white, with the heart-shaped Tombaugh Regio visible. Charon fills the right side — noticeably grey-blue with its distinctive dark reddish-brown polar cap (Mordor Macula) at the north pole. Faint orbital path lines trace their mutual orbit. The sheer size of Charon relative to Pluto is visually striking — not a tiny moon orbiting a planet, but two worlds locked in an eternal gravitational embrace. The Kuiper Belt's sparse icy bodies dot the background. Dual rim-lighting on both bodies from the distant Sun, balanced composition. Color palette: warm tan-white Pluto, cool grey-blue Charon, dark red-brown Mordor Macula, barycenter glow white, infinite black void. 8K, NASA visualization quality meets cinematic grandeur.

## 9. `bg07_pluto_atmosphere.webp` — Section 7: जादुई वायुमंडल
**Context:** Pluto has a thin atmosphere with blue haze layers that collapses and freezes when Pluto moves away from the Sun
> Mesmerizing cinematic backlit portrait of Pluto, photographed from behind with the Sun directly behind the dwarf planet, revealing its hauntingly beautiful atmosphere. Multiple distinct layers of blue photochemical haze glow luminously around Pluto's dark silhouette like nested halos — extending over 200 kilometers above the surface. The haze layers are created by sunlight breaking apart methane molecules high in the atmosphere. The surface below is in deep shadow but faint details are visible from atmospheric scattering. The image captures the impossible beauty of this moment — a world 5.9 billion kilometers from the Sun, barely lit, yet wearing a crown of ethereal blue light. This atmosphere is temporary — as Pluto's 248-year orbit carries it further from the Sun, this entire sky will freeze and fall as snow. Dramatic backlighting, volumetric haze layers, silhouette composition. Color palette: luminous cyan-blue haze layers, dark Pluto silhouette, brilliant white Sun backlight, deep space black, faint tan surface hints. 8K, New Horizons iconic image reimagined in Hollywood cinematography.
