## 4. `bg02_earth_atmosphere.webp` — Section 2: सुरक्षा का कवच
**Context:** Earth's atmosphere — ozone layer blocking UV, protecting life, 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen
> Dramatic cinematic cross-section visualization of Earth's atmospheric layers. The planet's curvature is visible at the bottom, with distinct atmospheric layers rising above: the troposphere (weather and clouds), stratosphere (ozone layer glowing faintly violet-blue as a protective shield), mesosphere, thermosphere with aurora effects, and finally the exosphere fading into space. Harmful UV radiation from the Sun is shown as damaging purple rays being absorbed and blocked by the ozone layer, with safe visible light passing through. The layers are color-coded and slightly visible. The mood conveys the shield protecting all life below. Volumetric atmospheric layers, UV ray visualization, aurora at high altitude. Color palette: blue troposphere, protective violet ozone layer, incoming purple UV rays, warm sunlight gold, deep black space above. 8K, scientific visualization meets cinematic drama.

## 6. `bg04_earth_magnetic.webp` — Section 4: चुम्बकीय ढाल
**Context:** Earth's magnetic field — generated by liquid iron core, deflects solar wind, enables life
> Spectacular cinematic visualization of Earth's magnetosphere protecting the planet. The solar wind — streams of charged golden-orange particles from the Sun — slams into Earth's magnetic field, dramatically deflected in a bow shock. Field lines arch from pole to pole in glowing electric blue, creating the invisible shield that makes life possible. On the night side, particles funnel along field lines toward the poles, creating shimmering aurora effects in green and violet. The compressed dayside and elongated night-side tail of the magnetosphere are clearly visible. Earth glows safely within its invisible fortress. Volumetric particle flows, magnetic field line glow, aurora at poles. Color palette: golden solar wind, electric blue magnetic field lines, green-violet aurora at poles, blue planet Earth, deep black space. 8K, NASA magnetosphere visualization quality.

## 11. `bg09_earth_pale_blue_dot.webp` — Section 9: Pale Blue Dot
**Context:** Carl Sagan's "Pale Blue Dot" — Voyager 1 photo from 6 billion km, Earth as a mote of dust
> Profoundly cinematic recreation of Carl Sagan's Pale Blue Dot moment — the most humbling photograph ever taken. The Voyager 1 spacecraft is small in the frame, its dish antenna pointing back toward the inner solar system, a beam of sunlight crossing the frame. In the center of that sunbeam, barely visible, is Earth — a tiny pale blue mote suspended in a sunbeam against the vast darkness of space. The image is intentionally small — the entire human history, every person ever born, every empire, war, achievement, contained in that barely-visible speck. Surrounding Voyager is only infinite black void. The mood is existential and profound — perspective beyond words. Film grain for emotional texture, deliberate minimalism, sunbeam across frame. Color palette: deep black void dominant, single pale blue dot, warm sunbeam gold diagonal, silver Voyager spacecraft. 8K but intentionally sparse, Carl Sagan documentary visual poetry.
