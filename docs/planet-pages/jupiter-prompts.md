## 5. `bg03_jupiter_cloud_bands.webp` — Section 3: रंगीन बादलों की पट्टियाँ
**Context:** Jupiter's cloud bands — ammonia ice, ammonium hydrosulfide, water clouds at different depths creating different colors
> Mesmerizing cinematic cross-section visualization of Jupiter's atmospheric chemistry. A vertical slice through the cloud layers reveals the chemistry creating the colors: the highest white ammonia ice clouds, then cream ammonium hydrosulfide layers, then darker water and organic compound clouds below giving amber-brown colors. The alternating jet streams flowing in opposite directions create the characteristic band pattern seen from above. The visualization is part scientific diagram, part art — showing the hidden complexity behind Jupiter's beauty. Color palette: white upper ammonia clouds, cream mid-layers, amber-orange lower clouds, dark brown-red deep atmosphere, subtle chemical molecule overlays. 8K, scientific visualization meets cinematic art.
